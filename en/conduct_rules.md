# Classroom Conduct Rules

## Prohibited

- Making noise or distracting other students and the instructor during
  class.
- Disturbing other students or the instructor (including with
  conversations unrelated to the class).
- Playing games on laptops or phones for entertainment.
- Watching vlogs, videos, TikTok, or other entertainment content.
- Engaging in off-task activities unrelated to the class in general.

## Permitted

- Working on assignments for other subjects and courses during class, as
  long as it doesn't disturb others. This does not exempt you from
  completing the required amount of work for this course ("Design of
  Intelligent Systems Software") by the deadlines — see `grade.md`.
- Developing games and testing them, when this is part of completing a
  lab assignment (for example, the variants in the "1.5 Games" section of
  `lab1.md`) or another course task, rather than entertainment.

## Consequences of Violations

- Violating any of the rules listed under "Prohibited" is grounds for
  assigning an extra lab assignment (see `additional_labs.md`).
- A fourth or later unexcused absence is also grounds for assigning an
  extra lab assignment.
