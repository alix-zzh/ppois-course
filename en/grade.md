# Grading Criteria

## Admission Condition and Bonuses

- **10** — all lab assignments submitted, not during the last 2 class
  sessions but well in advance, plus the extra assignment completed.
- **9** — all lab assignments submitted, but not during the last 2 class
  sessions.
- **8** — 3 of 4 lab assignments submitted before December 1.
- **7** — 3 of 4 lab assignments submitted after December 1.
- **6** — 2 of 4 lab assignments submitted, the third presented but not
  fully completed.
- **5** — 2 of 4 lab assignments submitted before December 1.
- **4** — 2 of 4 lab assignments submitted after December 1.
- **Not admitted** — fewer than 2 lab assignments submitted.

## Ten-Point Scale

The ten-point knowledge assessment scale comprises 7 groups of criteria,
ranked as follows.

### 10 points — Excellent (outstanding)

- systematic, deep, and complete knowledge of all sections of the
  curriculum, as well as of key questions beyond it;
- precise use of scientific terminology (including in a foreign language),
  stylistically correct and logically sound presentation of answers;
- flawless command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them
  effectively in posing and solving scientific and professional problems;
- a pronounced ability to solve complex problems independently and
  creatively in non-standard situations;
- full and deep mastery of the core and supplementary literature
  recommended by the curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the theories, concepts, and directions of the
  discipline and give them a critical assessment, drawing on achievements
  from other disciplines;
- creative, independent work in seminars and labs, active participation in
  group discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 9 points — Excellent

- systematic, deep, and complete knowledge of all sections of the
  curriculum;
- precise use of scientific terminology (including in a foreign language),
  stylistically correct and logically sound presentation of answers;
- command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them effectively in
  posing and solving scientific and professional problems;
- the ability to solve complex problems independently and creatively in
  non-standard situations within the curriculum, full mastery of the core
  and supplementary literature recommended by the curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the main theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and give them a critical assessment;
- independent work in seminars and labs, creative participation in group
  discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 8 points — Very good (nearly excellent)

- systematic, deep, and complete knowledge on all questions posed, within
  the scope of the curriculum;
- use of scientific terminology, stylistically correct and logically sound
  presentation of answers, the ability to draw well-founded conclusions;
- command of the discipline's tools (comprehensive analysis methods,
  information technology techniques), the ability to use them in posing
  and solving scientific and professional problems; the ability to solve
  complex problems independently within the curriculum;
- mastery of the core and supplementary literature recommended by the
  curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the main theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and give them a critical assessment;
- active independent work in seminars and labs, systematic participation
  in group discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 7 points — Good (very good)

- systematic, deep, and complete knowledge of all sections of the
  curriculum;
- use of scientific terminology (including in a foreign language),
  linguistically and logically correct presentation of answers, the
  ability to draw well-founded conclusions;
- command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them in posing and
  solving scientific and professional problems;
- mastery of the core and supplementary literature recommended by the
  curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the main theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and give them a critical assessment;
- independent work in seminars and labs, participation in group
  discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 6 points — Good

- sufficiently complete and systematic knowledge within the curriculum;
- use of the necessary scientific terminology, stylistically correct and
  logically sound presentation of answers, the ability to draw
  well-founded conclusions;
- command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them in solving
  academic and professional problems; the ability to independently apply
  standard (typical) solutions within the curriculum;
- mastery of the core literature recommended by the curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the basic theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and give them a comparative assessment; active
  independent work in seminars and labs, periodic participation in group
  discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 5 points — Fairly good

- sufficient knowledge within the curriculum;
- use of scientific terminology, stylistically correct and logically sound
  presentation of answers, the ability to draw conclusions;
- command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them in solving
  academic and professional problems;
- the ability to independently apply standard (typical) solutions within
  the curriculum;
- mastery of the core literature recommended by the curriculum;
- the ability to navigate the basic theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and give them a comparative assessment;
- independent work in seminars and labs, participation in group
  discussions, a high standard of work presentation.

### 4 points — Satisfactory (pass)

- a sufficient amount of knowledge within the educational standard;
- mastery of the core literature recommended by the curriculum;
- use of scientific terminology, stylistic and logical presentation of
  answers, the ability to draw conclusions without significant errors;
- command of the discipline's tools, the ability to use them in solving
  standard (typical) problems;
- the ability to solve standard (typical) problems under the instructor's
  guidance;
- the ability to navigate the main theories, concepts, and directions of
  the discipline and assess them;
- work under the instructor's guidance in seminars and labs, an acceptable
  standard of work presentation.

### 3 points — Unsatisfactory (fail)

- an insufficiently complete amount of knowledge within the educational
  standard;
- knowledge of part of the core literature recommended by the curriculum;
- use of scientific terminology, presentation of answers with significant
  linguistic and logical errors;
- weak command of the discipline's tools, incompetence in solving standard
  (typical) problems;
- inability to navigate the main theories, concepts, and directions of the
  discipline;
- passivity in seminars and labs, a low standard of work presentation.

### 2 points — Unsatisfactory

- fragmentary knowledge within the educational standard;
- knowledge of only a few literary sources recommended by the curriculum;
- inability to use the discipline's scientific terminology, gross
  stylistic and logical errors in answers;
- passivity in seminars and labs, a low standard of work presentation.

### 1 point — Unsatisfactory

- no knowledge or competencies within the educational standard, or refusal
  to answer.
