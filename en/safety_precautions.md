# Computer Lab Safety Rules

## Main Hazards

- Electric current (the PC and peripherals are powered at 220 V).
- Electromagnetic radiation and excessive eye strain.
- Fire hazard due to the high density of electrical equipment.

## Strictly Prohibited

- Bringing food and drinks into computer labs: spilled liquid causes short
  circuits and damages equipment.
- Plugging in personal gadgets, cables, or flash drives to outlets, system
  units, or the network infrastructure without the instructor's permission.
- Touching connectors, cables, power cords, and protective grounding.
- Touching the back of the system unit or the monitor.
- Placing personal items (bags, notebooks, clothing) on monitors, system
  units, or keyboards — this blocks ventilation and leads to component
  overheating.
- Moving powered-on equipment or attempting to fix faults yourself.

## Before Starting Work

- Leave your coat in the cloakroom and put bulky bags in the designated
  spot in the classroom.
- Check that cables, outlets, the PC case, the mouse, and the keyboard show
  no visible damage.
- Take your workstation as directed by the instructor or lab assistant.
- Adjust the chair height and the monitor's tilt. The optimal eye-to-screen
  distance is 50–70 cm.

## While Working

- Work calmly, without sudden movements, and don't distract your
  classmates.
- Press keys gently; avoid striking the keyboard or other input devices.
- Maintain good posture: your back should rest against the chair back, feet
  flat on the floor.
- Take breaks to rest your eyes (every 45–50 minutes of work, it's
  recommended to stretch or look out the window).

## In an Emergency

- If you notice a fault (a burning smell, an unusual noise, sparking, a
  sensation of electric current), stop working immediately, unplug the PC
  if it's safe to do so, and notify the instructor or the duty engineer.
- In case of an accident (electric shock, a cut, etc.), immediately notify
  the instructor so first aid can be given and medical help called.
- In case of fire, don't panic: on the instructor's command, leave the
  computer lab in an orderly manner following the evacuation plan posted on
  the building floor.

## At the End of the Session

- Close all programs and shut down the operating system properly.
- Tidy up your workstation and push in your chair.
- Remove trash and personal belongings.
